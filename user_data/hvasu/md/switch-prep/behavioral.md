# Behavioral

1.  Describe an unclear requirement you resolved. What did you do first to clarify it?
    
2.  Tell me about an engineering decision you're proud of. What alternatives did you consider, and why did you choose your approach?
    
3.  Tell me about a time you disagreed with a technical decision. How did you handle it?
    
4.  Describe a production bug that was difficult to diagnose. How did you systematically find the root cause?
    
5.  Tell me about a technical decision you made with incomplete information. What assumptions did you make?
    
6.  Describe a piece of code or architecture you inherited that was problematic. What did you change and why?
    
7.  Tell me about a time you chose a simple solution over a more sophisticated one. Why?
    
8.  Describe a technical mistake you made. How did you discover and fix it?
    
9.  Tell me about a time performance became a problem. How did you identify the bottleneck?
    
10.  Describe a situation where requirements changed significantly during implementation. How did you adapt?
     
11.  Tell me about a time you had to prioritize between multiple engineering tasks. How did you decide what to do first?
     
12.  Describe a situation where you had to balance technical debt against delivering a feature. What did you choose and why?
     
13.  Tell me about a time you improved an existing system without changing its external behavior.
     
14.  Describe a time when your initial technical approach turned out to be wrong. What made you change your mind?
     
15.  Tell me about a difficult debugging problem where the obvious explanation turned out to be incorrect.
     
16.  Describe an engineering trade-off you made between reliability, performance, complexity, and development time. Why did you make it?
     
17.  Tell me about a time you had to explain a technical problem or trade-off to a non-technical stakeholder.
     
18.  Describe a time you identified a potential production problem before it actually happened. What did you do?
     
19.  Tell me about a time you had to work with an unfamiliar codebase or technology. How did you get productive quickly?
     
20.  **What's one engineering decision you've made that you would defend strongly today? Walk me through the context, alternatives, trade-offs, and outcome.**
